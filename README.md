Belos
Smarter scheduling and routing for non-emergency medical transport.

Belos is a dispatch dashboard and scheduling prototype built for HackRPI 2026. It helps transport teams organize preplanned patient pickups, assign available ambulances, visualize their routes, and respond when a vehicle is delayed or taken out of service.

Unlike standard 911 dispatch software, Belos focuses on routine, scheduled transportation, such as hospital discharges, interfacility transfers, and trips to or from care facilities. The goal is to serve more scheduled pickups on time while reducing unnecessary driving and dispatcher workload.
